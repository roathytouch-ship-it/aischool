# Math Monster (offline Play)

Home card next to Word Guess in `index.html`. Not a subject. Not a Pass. No Start slot. No LLM.

## Who sees it

Everyone on Home. Label: **Made for Grades 4–8**. Not hidden by grade. Any enrolled grade may play.

## Round

- 10 questions, 3 lives, A–D multiple choice
- Default **Medium**. Easy / Medium / Hard
- + − × ÷ or mixed
- Score + streak bonus
- Lives out or 10 done → score screen
- Play again stays on the same screen

## Difficulty (tweaked 2026-09-05)

**Easy** — still G4 breathing room. Larger single-step + a few missing-number items (`7 × ? = 84`).

**Medium** — mixed rounds mix:
- two-step (`3 × 8 + 12`)
- parentheses `(9 + 3) × 4`
- missing number
- bigger single-step ranges

**Hard** — same plus **order-of-operations traps** (`5 + 4 × 3` = 17, not 27). Wrong A–D choices use the common mistakes (left-to-right, forgot brackets).

Wrong choices sit close to the real answer or match a typical slip so guessing is harder.

## Extra question types (random mix)

About 30–50% of a round (more on Hard) can be one of these instead of plain + − × ÷:

1. Compare — `12 × 8 ? 100` pick `>` `<` `=`
2. True / False — `7 × 8 = 54 ?`
3. Missing sign — `6 □ 3 = 18`
4. Monster HP — `👾 HP 48. Hit 3 × 12. HP left?`
5. Pattern — `4, 8, 16, ?`
6. Remainder — `17 ÷ 5` leaves remainder `2` (Medium/Hard only)

Easy still leans on plain arithmetic so Grade 4 is not dumped into remainders.
