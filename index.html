❓ FAQ

Is Live Talk in Silver or Gold?

No. Silver and Gold include AI Voice (and Gold has Review Pool). Live Talk is a separate 1h or 2h pack you buy only if you want it.

What happens to remaining minutes if I buy again?

They stay. The new pack minutes are added on top. Expiry becomes 90 days from the new purchase (your leftover time is still usable until that new date).

When does Live Talk expire?

90 days after each purchase. Profile shows the expiry date and how much of your pack is left.

Can I get a refund?

No cash refund for Live Talk packs (same idea as other credits). Use the minutes before they expire.

What is AI Voice vs Live Talk?

Device voice (Basic, 🔈) = phone reads replies free. AI Voice (Silver/Gold, 🎧 purple) = clearer AI voice; if used up, falls back to device voice with a short notice. Call is Live Talk (later). Mic next to the box is for you to speak into text.

How do I unlock French or Spanish?

French (teacher Étoile) and Spanish (teacher Estrella). Silver and Gold can study anytime. On Basic, upgrade or buy a Live Talk pack (1h/2h).

Do Subject Passes include Live Talk?

No. Subject Pass unlocks a subject for study (text). Live Talk is always a separate pack.
