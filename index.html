❓ FAQ

What is Chat vs AI Voice?

Chat is lesson text. AI Voice (Silver/Gold + language/Bee Pass) is clearer teacher speech — turn Auto voice on or tap Play. If the pool is empty, you get a soft note and free device voice until midnight. Basic is free device voice only.

How does the mic work?

Hold the mic next to the message box. Words appear as text — check, then Send. Same on every plan. If clearer mic runs out, free mic still works. Typing always works.

How do I unlock French, Spanish, or Russian?

French (Étoile), Spanish (Estrella), Russian (Ksenia). Silver and Gold include languages — no extra Pass. On Basic, buy a language Subject Pass ($7) or upgrade plan.

What is Subject Pass?

Basic only. Opens one extra subject for the month (45 min blocks · Reflect 10). Max 4 · Coding/AI $5 · FR/ES/RU $7 · Bee $6 · other $3.50. Silver/Gold already have all subjects.

When do daily pools reset?

At midnight, Asia/Phnom_Penh. Silver AI Voice ≈15 min TTS / 8 min mic · Gold ≈28 / 18. Empty → free device voice until then.
